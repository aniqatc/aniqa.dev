## Idea

I wanted a painted scene, seen from an isometric view, of the place that's been my home for the past few years and the street where we regularly take walks as a family. We walk these roads regardless of season, time of day or weather, so the scene had to include all of those. I also wanted to show our family's progression, from just the two of us to the three of us with our son.

The scene has two sets of controls: one to move through the times in our lives, and another to change the scene around us.

## Notes

1. The timeline runs from May 2024, when we moved in, to December 2026. It opens with a pink moving truck and boxes all over the lawn, then it's the two of us walking. A bump appears in October 2025, blue balloons go up when our son arrives at the end of June 2026, and after that it's a newborn in a blue swaddle, then a stroller.
2. The timeline's pause holds the date while the walk, the weather and every control carry on. The Pause under the scene stops everything.
3. Our outfits follow the season and the weather, down to the umbrella.
4. Seasons ripple out from wherever we are on the road, bringing new trees, new ground, and leaves, petals or snow.
5. Time of day is one wash over the whole scene, with longer shadows at dawn and dusk, and lamps and windows lit at night.
6. Wind and weather have their own controls: wind sways the trees and blows the leaves, and the weather brings fog, rain, snow or a storm.
7. The street is busy too. A neighbor walks a dog, a car drives in and parks, a bunny hops across the road by the stop sign, and deer wander through the woods now and then. Tap a tree to shake some of its leaves off.
8. The community clubhouse sits at the far end of the houses. We've spent time there playing pool, getting coffee and using the gym.
9. I built it with Claude Fable 5.2 from photos of our community: aerial shots for the layout, and shots from our point of view on the walk. Then came lots of tweaking, with details I added by hand: the stop sign, our car, the street lights, the neighbor with the dog and a few other small touches.
10. In Fullscreen you can pinch, drag or scroll to zoom around the scene. With reduced motion on, the timeline starts held on one date.
