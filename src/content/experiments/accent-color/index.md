## Idea

I wanted to see how far `accent-color` could take native form controls, and how the same color looked across light and dark surfaces.

## Notes

1. `accent-color` recolors checkboxes, radios, range inputs and progress bars without replacing their native behavior.
2. Browsers adjust the check mark to keep it visible against a light accent. Try the pale yellow preset to see it switch to a darker mark.
